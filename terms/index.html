Our services

Magic Events organises and sets up private events across the United Arab Emirates, including birthday parties for children and adults, wedding anniversaries, marriage proposals, graduation celebrations, gender reveals, baby showers, Eid and family gatherings, and general private parties.

Depending on the package you select, our service may include:

  • Themed decorations styled to your chosen concept and colour palette
  • Balloon garlands, installations and backdrops
  • Dessert, food, drinks and cake table styling
  • Lighting, including string and fairy lights
  • Table arrangements and settings
  • Coordination and arrangement of the venue space

The exact items, quantities and scope included in your event will be those set out in the quotation or package confirmed with you in writing. Anything not expressly listed is not included.

Cancellation by you

If you need to cancel, please tell us in writing as soon as possible. The following charges apply, calculated on the total fee for your event:

These charges reflect the fact that we reserve your date exclusively, turn away other bookings, and purchase materials, custom items and perishable stock specifically for your event well ahead of the day itself.

We design our installations to look their best for the duration of your event, but we cannot guarantee the lifespan or condition of balloons beyond the time of handover, particularly outdoors or in hot conditions.

Colours of balloons, flowers, fabrics and printed items may vary slightly from images, samples or mock-ups shown to you, due to differences in materials, batches and screen displays. Fresh flowers and seasonal items are subject to availability, and we may substitute an item of equivalent quality and appearance where necessary.

Setups are designed to be decorative. They are not play equipment and should not be climbed on, pulled or moved.
